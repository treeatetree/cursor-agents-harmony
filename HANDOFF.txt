## 架构速查

```
entry/src/main/ets/
  api/          CursorApiClient, RunSseClient, SseParser, StreamCoordinator, ApiUnwrap
  viewmodels/   InboxVM, DetailVM, NewAgentVM, SessionVM
  pages/        Index(Inbox), NewAgentPage, AgentDetailPage, SettingsPage, InAppBrowserPage(备用)
  components/   AgentRow, ComposerBar, OutcomeStrip, MarkdownLite, ToolCallRow, …
  store/        ApiKeyStore(HUKS), AgentCache, ThemeStore, PrefStore, BreakpointStore
  models/       Api.ets, Timeline.ets, Sse.ets
```

关键链路：
- 列表：`GET /v1/agents`
- 对话：`GET /v0/agents/{id}/conversation`（Bearer；详情时间线主数据源，不是只靠 runs）
- 运行/结果：`GET .../runs` + SSE `.../stream`；OutcomeStrip 用 git.branches / prUrl
- 创建：`POST /v1/agents`；追问：`POST .../runs`
